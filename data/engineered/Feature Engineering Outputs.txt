Feature-Engineering Outputs
1. engineered_ethnic_composition_full.csv
	A temporally expanded and harmonized ethnic composition dataset, derived from cleaned UN/census data.

	Represents
		A historical ethnic composition panel, still grounded in observed data but made model-ready through 			interpolation.
		The primary target variable source for forecasting (ethnic shares).

	Feature Engineering
		Interpolated ethnic shares across missing years to form a continuous time series.
		Aligned ethnic composition to a common time axis (e.g. 2000–2024).
		Ensured consistent ethnic group coverage per country.
		Standardized country and ethnic identifiers.

2. ims_destination_features.csv
	An engineered migration feature table describing destination-country migration characteristics.

	Represents
		Pull-side structural migration features.
		Long-run migration attractiveness or exposure of destination countries.

	Feature Engineering
		Aggregated IMS migrant stock by destination country.
		Derived destination-side indicators (e.g. total migrant stock, growth trends, shares).
		Standardized time intervals and country identifiers.

3. ims_od_inflow_outflow_engineered.csv
	An origin–destination (OD) migration feature table, engineered from IMS stock data.

	Represents
		Directional migration pressure between origin and destination countries.
		Structural migration dynamics, not event-level movements.

	Feature Engineering
		Constructed origin–destination migration relationships.
		Derived inflow and outflow proxies from stock changes.
		Aggregated flows across consistent temporal windows.

4. ims_origin_features.csv
	An engineered migration feature table describing origin-country migration characteristics.

	Represents
		Push-side structural migration features.
		Long-run emigration pressure from origin countries.

	Feature Engineering
		Aggregated IMS migrant stock by origin country.
		Derived origin-side indicators (e.g. emigration intensity, growth, dispersion).
		Standardized country identifiers and temporal structure.
